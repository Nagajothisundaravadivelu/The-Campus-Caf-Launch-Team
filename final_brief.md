**Subject:** Decision Brief: Campus Café Pilot & Financial Audit

## Recommendation

Approve the budget and verify local operating costs, including menu prices and supplier costs, before starting an 8-week pilot offering one plant-based milk option. Use the pilot to measure demand; do not treat projected volume as established.

## Financial Model

These are planning estimates based on unverified assumptions: average order value of **₹120**, variable cost of **₹54 per order**, monthly fixed costs of **₹260,000**, and **22 operating days per month**.

- **Contribution per order:** ₹120 − ₹54 = **₹66**
- **Monthly break-even:** ₹260,000 ÷ ₹66 = 3,939.39, rounded up to **3,940 orders**
- **Daily break-even:** 3,940 ÷ 22 = 179.09, approximately **180 orders per operating day**
- **Target-case projected monthly operating surplus:** (240 orders/day × 22 days × ₹66) − ₹260,000 = **₹88,480** at 240 orders/day

The projected surplus is not guaranteed profit. Startup costs, taxes, depreciation, financing, spoilage, and other unmodelled costs may reduce it.

## Market Evidence and Risks

- **Plant-based dairy interest:** The Ipsos 2025 report says **51% of 220 surveyed Indian plant-based-food consumers** intended to increase consumption of plant-based dairy over the next 6–12 months. This survey does not establish student demand or environmental benefits. [Read the Ipsos report](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/publication/documents/2025-03/Ipsos_PBFIA_Plant%20based%20foods%20report%202025.pdf).
- **Local pricing and costs:** Nearby café menu prices and supplier costs have not been verified. Obtain local menu data and supplier quotations before starting the pilot.
- **Unverified demand:** No campus-specific demand data is available. The 180 daily orders required for break-even are a planning estimate, not verified student demand. The ratio of 180 daily break-even orders to total enrollment of 8,000 is **2.25%**; this is not a measured customer conversion rate and does not mean that 2.25% of unique students will purchase daily.

## Next Steps

After budget approval and local cost verification, run the 8-week pilot. Track daily transactions, average order value, contribution margin, food waste, and customer feedback. Review actual results and verified costs before deciding whether to expand.
